3. Your footage

This is the part most people are actually asking about, so it comes first.

  • The clip stays on your computer. The Antidote application repairs your file on your own machine, against the original file on your own disk. We do not receive, store, stream or copy the clip.
  • The engine runs on our servers. During a repair, the application exchanges instructions and structural information about the file with our servers — for example the layout of atoms and frames, byte offsets, sizes and camera metadata — never picture or sound data.
  • The diagnosis sample. To diagnose a failure we ask you to send a sample of roughly two megabytes extracted from the damaged file. A sample can contain fragments of picture data. It is used only to diagnose and quote that job, is accessible only to the engineer working on it, and is deleted when the job closes (see section 6).
  • Footage can be personal data. If your footage shows identifiable people, a sample may contain their personal data. You confirm that you are entitled to send it to us for repair; we process it only on your instruction and for that purpose.

5. Legal bases

Under the Armenian Data Protection Law we process personal data with your consent, which you give by sending us a request, a sample or a message for the stated purpose, and on the other grounds the Law provides, including where processing is necessary to perform a contract with you or to meet a legal obligation. Where the GDPR applies, the bases are:

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) — diagnosing, quoting, repairing and delivering; support.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) — accounting and tax records.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) — keeping the site and the application secure, fixing bugs, defending legal claims. We have weighed these against your interests and do not believe they override them.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) — anything optional, such as a newsletter if we ever start one. You can withdraw consent at any time without affecting what was done before.

Our servers are located in [country / provider]. Because we serve customers in many countries, your data may be transferred across borders.

From Armenia. 9. Security

The strongest protection we offer is architectural: the clip never leaves your computer, so there is no copy of it for anyone to lose. For what we do hold, we use encryption in transit and at rest, access limited to the engineer working on a job, logged access to production systems, and separation between working and backup storage. Samples are stored in a segregated area that is purged on schedule. Employees and contractors are under written confidentiality obligations, and our systems are configured to the requirements the Government of the Republic of Armenia prescribes for information systems that hold personal data.

10. Your rights

Under the Armenian Data Protection Law, and under the GDPR where it applies, you can:

  • know whether we hold personal data about you, and get a copy of it and of the purposes, sources and recipients;
  • correct data that is incomplete, inaccurate or out of date;
  • delete or block data that is no longer needed or was processed unlawfully;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests, and withdraw consent where processing is based on it;
  • restrict processing while a dispute is resolved, and receive data you gave us in a portable form (GDPR);
  • complain to a supervisory authority (see section 15).

Write to privacy@your-domain.com. We answer within [30] days, and where the law sets a shorter period — the Armenian Data Protection Law requires unlawful processing to be corrected or the data destroyed within three working days — we meet it. We may ask you to confirm your identity before acting. Exercising your rights is free.

11. Cookies and local storage

This website sets no cookies. It stores one value in your browser's local storage — your choice of dark or light theme — which never leaves your device. We do not use analytics or advertising trackers. If that changes, this section will change first and we will ask for consent where the law requires it.

12. If something goes wrong

If personal data leaks from our systems we will publish a notice, report it to the Personal Data Protection Agency and to the Police of the Republic of Armenia as the Armenian Data Protection Law requires, notify the affected people without undue delay, and, where the GDPR applies, notify the competent supervisory authority within 72 hours. If we discover unlawful processing we will stop it and fix or destroy the data within three working days and tell you.

13. Children

Antidote is a service for working videographers and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has sent us data, contact us and we will delete it.
